# Returns & Exchanges Policy

## Return Policy Overview
We want you to love your saree! If you are not completely satisfied, Narees offers a hassle-free 7-day Return & Exchange policy from the date of delivery.

## Return Eligibility Criteria
1. The return request must be raised within **7 calendar days** of receiving the delivery.
2. The item must be unused, unwashed, unaltered, and undamaged.
3. All original tags, price tags, silk mark authenticity tags, and packaging must remain intact.
4. Blouses that have been stitched, customized, or altered are not eligible for return.
5. Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Clearance" are non-returnable unless defective.

## Damaged or Wrong Product Received
- If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect saree, please inform us within **48 hours** of delivery.
- You can upload photos/unboxing video via the Returns portal or chat assistant.
- We will arrange a free reverse pickup and provide a full refund or free replacement.

## Exchange Process
- You can exchange a saree for a different color, design, or saree type.
- Reverse pickup is arranged by Narees free of cost for the first exchange.
- Replacement sarees are dispatched as soon as the returned product passes quality check at our warehouse (24-48 hours after pickup).
