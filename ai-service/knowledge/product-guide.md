# Saree Care & Product Guide

## Fabric Care & Maintenance
- **Silk Sarees (Kanchipuram, Banarasi, Paithani)**: Dry clean only. Never machine wash. Store in clean muslin/cotton cloth wrapped loosely. Avoid direct sunlight.
- **Cotton & Linen Sarees**: Gentle hand wash in cold water with mild detergent. Dry in shade.
- **Georgette & Chiffon Sarees**: Soft hand wash or dry clean. Iron at low temperature setting.

## Blouse & Petticoat Information
- All sarees come with an unstitched blouse piece included unless specified.
- Silk sarees include Silk Mark certification of pure silk authenticity.
