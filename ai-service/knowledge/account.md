# Customer Account Guide

## Managing Your Account
- **Profile Details**: Update name, phone number, and default shipping addresses under Profile Settings.
- **Wishlist**: Save favorite sarees for later by clicking the heart icon on product pages.
- **Order History**: View all current, past, returned, or cancelled orders with downloadable invoices.

## Registration & Login
- New customers register with Email, Username, Password, and role.
- An OTP is sent to your email to verify your account registration.
- Login using either your registered Username or Email address with Password.

## Password Reset
- Click "Forgot Password" on the login page.
- Enter your registered email address to receive a 6-digit verification OTP.
- Enter the OTP and your new password to reset account access safely.
