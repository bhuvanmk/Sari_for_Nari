# Payment Assistance & Methods

## Accepted Payment Methods
Narees supports multiple safe and seamless payment options powered by Razorpay:
1. **UPI Payment**: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Cred, and any UPI ID.
2. **Credit & Debit Cards**: Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Diners Club, and American Express.
3. **Net Banking**: All major Indian public and private banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, etc.).
4. **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Available nationwide up to ₹10,000 order value.

## Payment Failure / Amount Deducted Issue
If money was deducted from your account but the order failed or was not generated:
- Do not worry! Razorpay and banks handle payment reconciliations automatically.
- The deducted amount is auto-refunded to your bank account within **24 to 48 hours**.
- You can check payment status by providing your email or contacting our AI Chatbot Assistant.
