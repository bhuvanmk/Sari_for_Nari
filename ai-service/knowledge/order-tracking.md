# Order Tracking & Status Guide

## How to Track Your Order
1. **Via AI Assistant**: Simply ask "Track my order" or "Where is my order?" in the chat window while logged in.
2. **Via My Account**: Go to "My Orders" in the header navigation menu to view all past & present purchases.
3. **Via SMS / Email**: Click the tracking link sent in your dispatch notification SMS/email.

## Order Status Descriptions
- **Order Placed / Pending**: Order has been created successfully and received by warehouse.
- **Confirmed**: Payment verified, product packed and ready for courier pickup.
- **Shipped**: Courier has picked up the package and tracking number is active.
- **Out for Delivery**: Local delivery agent is delivering package today.
- **Delivered**: Package successfully handed over.
- **Cancelled**: Order was cancelled before shipment.
