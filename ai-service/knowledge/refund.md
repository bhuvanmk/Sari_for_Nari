# Refund Policy & Timelines

## Refund Mode & Processing Times
- **Online Payments (UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Cards, Net Banking)**: Refunded directly back to the payment account used during checkout. Processing time is 3 to 5 working days after refund initiation.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD) Returns**: Refunded via Bank Transfer (NEFT/IMPS) or UPI to bank account details provided during return request, or credited to Narees Store Credits within 2 business days.

## Refund Status & Tracking
- Customers receive instant confirmation via SMS & Email when a refund is initiated.
- Refund reference number (ARN/UTR number) is provided for tracking with your bank.
