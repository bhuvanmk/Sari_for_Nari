# Saree Shopping & Occasion Guide

## Choosing Sarees by Occasion
- **Weddings & Grand Receptions**: Heavy Silk sarees like Kanchipuram / Kanjivaram, Banarasi Brocade, Zari work organza, or Rich Paithani with traditional motifs.
- **Festivals (Diwali, Navratri, Durga Puja, Onam, Pongal)**: Bright Silk, Bandhani, Chanderi, Kasavu, or Tussar Silk in vibrant red, yellow, green, or royal blue.
- **Parties & Evening Events**: Designer Georgette, Chiffon with sequins, Net sarees, or Satin finish metallic weaves.
- **Workwear & Daily Wear**: Breathable Linens, Handloom Cottons, Bengal Tant, or light Printed Chanderi.
