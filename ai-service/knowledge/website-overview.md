# Narees Website Overview

Narees is a premier Indian e-commerce platform dedicated exclusively to exquisite sarees and ethnic fashion for women. We celebrate Indian heritage and craftsmanship by bringing traditional weaves, contemporary designs, and luxury saree collections directly to your doorstep.

## Our Collections & Categories
- **Silk Sarees**: Kanchipuram, Banarasi, Chanderi, Mysore Silk, Tussar Silk, Organza Silk, and Art Silk.
- **Cotton & Handloom**: Bengal Cotton, Linen Sarees, Khadi, Daily Wear Cottons.
- **Designer & Party Wear**: Georgette, Chiffon, Net, Sequins, Velvet Accent, and Bollywood Style Sarees.
- **Regional & Traditional**: Bandhani (Gujarat/Rajasthan), Paithani (Maharashtra), Patola, Kasavu (Kerala), Sambalpuri (Odisha).

- 100% Genuine Certified Weaves & Premium Fabrics.

- Cash on Delivery (COD) available nationwide.
