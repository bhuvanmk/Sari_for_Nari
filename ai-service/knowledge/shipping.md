# Shipping Policy & Delivery Information

## Delivery Locations
Narees delivers across all PIN codes in India. We also ship internationally to over 30 countries including USA, UK, Canada, UAE, Australia, and Singapore.

## Shipping Charges & Free Delivery
- **Standard Domestic Shipping**: Flat ₹99 for orders below ₹1,499.
- **Free Domestic Shipping**: FREE on all orders of ₹1,499 and above.
- **Express Shipping**: Available in select metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata) for flat ₹199.
- **International Shipping**: Calculated at checkout based on weight and country destination.

## Delivery Timelines
- **Metro Cities**: 2 to 4 business days.
- **Rest of India**: 4 to 7 business days.
- **Remote / Special Areas**: 7 to 10 business days.
- **Express Shipping**: 1 to 2 business days in supported metros.
- **International Shipping**: 7 to 14 business days via DHL/FedEx Express.

## Order Dispatch
- Orders are processed and dispatched within 24 to 48 hours of order confirmation.
- Orders placed on Sundays or National Holidays are dispatched on the next working day.
- Once dispatched, customers receive an SMS, WhatsApp notification, and email containing the tracking number and courier link.
