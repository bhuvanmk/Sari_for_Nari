# Order Cancellation Policy

## Cancellation Window
- You can cancel an order **anytime before it is shipped**.
- Orders in "Order Placed", "Confirmed", or "Processing" status can be cancelled immediately through your Narees account or via the AI Assistant ("Cancel My Order").

## Cancellation after Shipment
- Once an order enters "Shipped" or "Out for Delivery" status, it cannot be cancelled directly online.
- You can refuse delivery when the courier arrives, or initiate a standard return within 7 days of receiving the item.

## Refund on Cancellation
- **Prepaid Orders**: 100% full refund is automatically processed back to original source (UPI/Card/Bank) within 3-5 business days.
- **COD Orders**: No payment was collected, order is cancelled without charge.
- Stock is automatically restored to our store inventory immediately.
