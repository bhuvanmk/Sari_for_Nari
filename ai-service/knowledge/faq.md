# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Q: How can I search for sarees by price or color?
A: You can type in the search bar or ask our AI Assistant! For example, ask "Show red sarees under 3000" or "Show me wedding silk sarees".

## Q: Is Cash on Delivery (COD) available?
A: Yes, COD is available across India for orders up to ₹10,000.

## Q: Are your silk sarees authentic?
A: Yes, 100% of our pure silk sarees come with Silk Mark Organization of India (SMOI) certification tags.

## Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
A: Delivery address can be updated while your order is in "Order Placed" or "Confirmed" status. Ask the AI Assistant or go to My Orders.

## Q: How do I contact customer support?
A: You can chat with our Narees AI Assistant anytime, or email us at support@sareesfornaaris.com or call our toll-free helpline.
