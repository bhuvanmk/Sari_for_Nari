# Product Exchanges Policy

## How Exchanges Work
If you prefer a different color, fabric, or saree pattern:
1. Log into your Narees account or ask our AI Chatbot Assistant for exchange help.
2. Select your order and choose "Request Exchange".
3. Choose the replacement product from available catalog items.
4. Once requested, our courier partner will pick up the item within 48 hours.
5. After inspection at our center, the new item is shipped immediately.

## Price Difference Adjustment
- **If replacement saree is higher price**: Pay the difference via UPI/card online or COD.
- **If replacement saree is lower price**: The remaining balance is credited to your Narees Wallet or original payment source.
